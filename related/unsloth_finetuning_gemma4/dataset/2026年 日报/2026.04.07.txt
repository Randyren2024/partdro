Business Travel Itinerary: Shanghai – Deqing/Hangzhou - Yiwu – Guangzhou
Dates: April 8 – 15, 2026

Phase 1: Shanghai & Tech Hub (April 8–9)

April 8 (Wednesday): Arrival & Logistics

Arrival at Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG).

Accommodation: Da Zhong Airport Hotel (located within the airport).

Evening: Brief rest or preparation of documentation for meetings with drone manufacturers.

April 9 (Thursday): Drones & JTS Group

Daytime: Factory visits within a 100km radius of Shanghai (Suzhou/Kunshan or Songjiang areas).

Inspection of production lines: commercial, industrial, and agricultural drones.

Meeting with representatives from JTS Group (follow-up from trade fair discussions).

Evening: Transit to Yiwu.

Option: High-speed rail (G-Train) from Shanghai Hongqiao Station (approx. 1.5–2h).

Accommodation: Yiwu Yandoo Yayue Hotel (No. 599-1 Huigang Road).

Phase 2: Yiwu – Quality Control & Production (April 10)

April 10 (Friday): Inspection & New Orders

Daytime: Operations in Yiwu.

Quality verification of backpacks and other products from the current portfolio.

Development of the outline and technical specifications for a new order.

Evening: Travel to Guangzhou.

Recommendation: Flight from Yiwu (YIW) to Guangzhou (CAN) or a night high-speed train (prices are comparable).

Accommodation: Atour Hotel Guangzhou Baiyun Guanghuayi Road.

Phase 3: Guangzhou & Shenzhen – Key Suppliers (April 11–14)

April 11 – 13 (Saturday – Monday): Strategic Meetings

Meetings with key partners: Yakata and Emerson.

Commercial negotiations, establishing production timelines, and logistics planning.

Shenzhen (Optional): If time permits, a day trip to Shenzhen (approx. 30–45 min by high-speed rail from Guangzhou) to meet with DGU.

April 14 (Tuesday): Finalization & Departure

Final adjustments to orders and summary of visitation reports.

Evening: Transfer to Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN).

Night of April 14/15: Departure from China.
